INSIDE SCOOP Oracle Director Buys Stock After a 3-Year Gap BY ED LIN racle stock has backed away from a record, but shares of the software firm still sport recent gains. One Oracle direc- tor just bought shares for the first time in years. Oracle stock traded to a record of $198.31 on Dec. 9 before closing at $190.45. The next day, shares slid 7% to $177.74 when numbers for the No- vember quarter missed estimates. Shares remain near that price, and maintain a gain of about 60% for the latest 12 months. Director Charles “Wick” Moorman paid $950,000 on Feb. 12 for 5,500 Oracle shares, an average price of $172.35 each. He purchased the stock through a trust that now owns 52,954 shares, according to a filing Moorman made with the Securities and Ex- change Commission. Moorman also owns 35,589 Oracle shares in a per- sonal account. Oracle didn’t make Moorman available for comment, and the com- pany also declined to comment. Moorman, a senior advisor to Am- trak, and a former president and CEO of the railroad, has been an Oracle director since 2018. His last purchase of Oracle stock on the open market was more than three years ago. In January 2022, Moorman paid $1.3 million for 15,000 shares, an average price of $83.76 each. B Big Pension Ups Stakes In Palantir, Nvidia, Tesla, and Sells AT&T t’s said that everything’s bigger in Texas, and the state’s largest pub- lic pension just made some outsize moves, increasing stakes in a handful of the most volatile technol- ogy stocks. Teacher Retirement System of Texas scooped up shares of Palantir Technologies, Nvidia, and Tesla, and halved its investment in AT&T in the fourth quarter. TRS, as the pension is known, dis- closed the trades in a form it filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission. TRS declined to com- ment on the changes. The Texas pension managed total net assets of $210.5 billion as of Aug. 31, 2024, making it the U.S.’ sixth- largest public pension and one of the world’s top 25. Data-analytics company Palantir saw its stock rocket 340% in 2024, compared with a 23% rise in the S&P 500. TRS loaded up on Palantir, buy- ing 507,340 shares to end the fourth quarter with 520,900 shares. TRS also bought 1.8 million shares of chip maker Nvidia to end the quar- ter with 8.7 million. Nvidia stock soared 171% in 2024; so far this year, it’s up another up 3.4%. Electric-vehicle maker Tesla’s stock soared 63% in 2024 but is down 12% so far in 2025. The Texas pension sold 1.9 million worth of AT&T shares to end the quarter with 1.8 million. AT&T has been reporting strong earnings, in- cluding in the latest report in Janu- ary. The telecom’s dividend has lost its luster, but the stock still offers a hefty yield. And now that AT&T has largely left the media business, its shares have surged. AT&T stock rose 37% in 2024. 8 Oracle director Charles “Wick” Moorman bought $1 million of shares of the soft- ware company, his first open-market stock purchase since 2022. Increases in Holdings Zymeworks (ZYME) EcoRI Capital raised its stake in the oncology-focused biopharma- ceutical company to 15,720,161 shares. EcoRI Capital purchased 309,083 Zymeworks shares from Jan. 13 through Jan. 17, 2025, at per share prices ranging from $13.39 to $14.01. Adecoagro (AGRO) Tether Holdings raised its stake in the agricultural company to 20,398,699 shares. Tether Holdings purchased 350,450 Adecoagro shares from Jan. 3 through Jan. 17, 2025, at prices ranging from $9.56 to $10.01 each. Tether Holdings revealed an initial stake of 10,048,249 Adecoagro shares that it bought from July 29 through Aug. 16, 2024, at prices ranging from $9.45 to $11.66, and has been in- creasing its stake ever since. On Feb. 14, Tether Holdings made an offer to purchase 51% of Adeco- agro’s outstanding stock above market value for $12.41 per share in cash. Following the stock purchases, Tether Holdings owns 20.2% of Adecoagro’s outstanding shares. Shares of Adecoagro have gained roughly 12% since the beginning of this year and 9.7% since the offer was announced.
